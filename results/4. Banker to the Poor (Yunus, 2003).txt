  Aboutness Statement

  ▎ This autobiographical account by Muhammad Yunus, Bangladeshi economist and founder of the Grameen Bank, describes how he developed the system of           
  micro-credit to combat poverty. The narrative traces Yunus's personal journey from Chittagong through his education in America and back to Bangladesh, where 
  encounters with rural poverty in Jobra Village led him to pioneer small loans to the poor. The work chronicles the founding, growth, and challenges of the   
  Grameen Bank from pilot project through expansion into developing and developed countries, its evolution through the 1990s, and diversification into Grameen 
  Enterprises. Serves as both personal memoir and advocacy for microfinance as a tool for poverty eradication.
