  Aboutness Statement: This scholarly monograph examines Richard Baxter's (1615–1691) Trinitarian theological method as articulated in his Methodus theologiae. It
   traces the intellectual roots of Baxter's exemplaristic logic in medieval scholastic thought — particularly Scotism and Nominalism — and in early modern
  logical traditions (Ramist and Lullist logic). The work situates Baxter within the scholastic paradigm of "faith seeking understanding" and explores his
  catechetical theology. An advanced academic study for researchers in historical theology and early modern intellectual history.
