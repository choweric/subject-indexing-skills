❯ Title: Artificial black holes /\ 
\
Abstract: "Physicists are pondering the possibility of simulating black holes in the laboratory by means of various "analog models". These analog models,      
typically based on condensed matter physics, can be used to help us understand general relativity (Einstein's gravity); conversely, abstract techniques        
developed in general relativity can sometimes be used to help us understand certain aspects of condensed matter physics. This book contains 13 chapters -      
written by experts in general relativity, particle physics, and condensed matter physics - that explore various aspects of this two-way traffic."--Jacket.\

  Aboutness Statement

  ▎ This physics resource is an edited collection of research-level chapters exploring analog models of black holes — systems in condensed matter physics
  (superfluids, Bose-Einstein condensates, slow light) that simulate aspects of black hole physics in laboratory settings. The central theme is the
  bidirectional intellectual exchange between general relativity and condensed matter physics: analog models help test predictions such as Hawking radiation,
  while general relativity techniques illuminate condensed matter phenomena. Intended for researchers and advanced students in theoretical and condensed matter
   physics.
